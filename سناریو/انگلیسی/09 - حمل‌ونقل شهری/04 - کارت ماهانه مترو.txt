Nima: I want to buy a monthly pass for the metro, where should I go?
Employee: You can buy it at the ticket office, or at the machine near the entrance.
Nima: How much does the monthly pass cost, and does it cover all the lines?
Employee: The monthly pass is forty units, and it covers all metro lines without limits.
Nima: Can I use the pass for the bus as well, or is it only for the metro?
Employee: It is for the metro only, for the bus you need a separate pass.
Nima: Is the pass in the form of a card, or a paper ticket?
Employee: It is a rechargeable card, you can renew it each month at the same office.
Nima: Do I need to bring my identity document to buy the pass?
Employee: Yes, please bring your identity document, the pass is registered to you.
Nima: That is clear, I will go to the office and get the monthly pass now.
Employee: You are welcome, and the pass becomes valid from the first day of the month.
