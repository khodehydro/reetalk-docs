Passenger: When is the next train to Isfahan, please?
Agent: The next train leaves at ten thirty in the morning.
Passenger: Is there a train after that, in the afternoon?
Agent: Yes, there is another one at three in the afternoon, and one at six in the evening.
Passenger: How long does the journey take to Isfahan?
Agent: It takes about four hours by the express train.
Passenger: Are the seats reserved, or do we choose any seat on the train?
Agent: The seats are reserved, you can choose your seat when you buy the ticket.
Passenger: How much is a second-class ticket?
Agent: A second-class ticket is eight units, and first class is twelve.
Passenger: I will take a second-class ticket for the three o'clock train.
Agent: No problem, I will issue the ticket now, please wait a moment.
