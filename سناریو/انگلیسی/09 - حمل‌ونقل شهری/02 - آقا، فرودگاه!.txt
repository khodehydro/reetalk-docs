Traveler: Excuse me, could you take me to the airport, please?
Driver: Of course, the airport is about forty minutes from here.
Traveler: That is fine, how much will the fare be?
Driver: The fare is fixed at twenty units, whatever the route.
Traveler: That is reasonable, can you take the fastest route, I am in a hurry.
Driver: Yes, I will, but the airport road can be busy at this hour.
Traveler: Please try your best, I have a flight in an hour and a half.
Driver: I understand, we will leave right away, do you have luggage?
Traveler: Just this one suitcase, it is in the trunk.
Driver: Good, everything is set, we are on our way to the airport.
Traveler: Thank you, I really appreciate your help.
Driver: You are welcome, we have arrived at the airport entrance.
