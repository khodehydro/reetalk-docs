Sara: I need to change lines, which line should I take to reach the university?
Officer: From this station, take the red line for three stops, then change to the green line.
Sara: Where should I change, is there a direct connection between the lines?
Officer: Yes, the lines connect on the same platform, it is very easy to change.
Sara: How many stops is it on the green line, after the change?
Officer: It is four stops on the green line, then you get off at the university station.
Sara: Do I need a separate ticket for the change, or is one ticket enough?
Officer: One ticket is enough, you can change as many times as you want within the time limit.
Sara: That is convenient, and how long is the whole trip?
Officer: The whole trip takes about twenty five minutes, including the change.
Sara: Thank you for the clear directions, I understand now.
Officer: You are welcome, the signs in the station are also clear, follow the green line sign.
