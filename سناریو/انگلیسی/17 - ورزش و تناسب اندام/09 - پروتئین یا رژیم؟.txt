Mona: I have been following a training plan, and I want to discuss my diet with you.
Coach: Of course, what does your current diet look like, and what are your goals?
Mona: I eat regular meals, but I am not sure about protein, and whether I need supplements.
Coach: For training, protein is important for recovery, but food should remain your main source.
Mona: So I do not need protein powder, if I eat enough protein from real food?
Coach: Correct, chicken, fish, eggs, and beans provide good protein, without extra products.
Mona: What about carbohydrates, should I avoid them to stay fit, or are they useful?
Coach: Carbohydrates give you energy for training, so include them, especially before exercise.
Mona: And fats, should I cut them completely, or is some fat needed in the diet?
Coach: Do not cut fats completely, healthy fats from nuts, fish, and olive oil are necessary.
Mona: How many meals should I have, and should I eat something before or after training?
Coach: Three balanced meals, with a light snack before and after training, that is a good routine.
Mona: Is it important to drink enough water, and how much should I drink during exercise?
Coach: Yes, drink water regularly, and sip during training, to replace what you lose.
Mona: I will follow this, and I will keep my diet balanced around my training plan.
Coach: That is the right approach, a good diet supports training, and never replaces it.
