Leila: I have signed up for my first marathon, and I am preparing for the big day.
Coach: That is a wonderful goal, how many weeks do you have until the race?
Leila: I have ten weeks, and I follow a training plan with increasing distances each week.
Coach: A gradual increase is the right approach, and how do you feel after the long runs?
Leila: I feel tired but good, my body is adapting to the longer distances.
Coach: That is a positive sign, and remember to rest well and not to increase too fast.
Leila: What should I focus on in the last few weeks, to reach the race in good condition?
Coach: Reduce the distance slowly, keep the runs light, and let the body recover fully.
Leila: And my diet, should I change anything in the final weeks before the marathon?
Coach: Keep the same balanced diet, with extra carbohydrates in the last days, for energy.
Leila: Is there anything I should avoid before the race, to prevent problems on the day?
Coach: Avoid new foods and heavy meals on race day, and drink water, but not too much at once.
Leila: What about the clothes and shoes, should I use new ones on the race day?
Coach: Never wear anything new on race day, use the shoes and clothes you have trained in.
Leila: I will remember that, and I feel ready, though a little nervous.
Coach: The nerves are normal, you have prepared well, and enjoy every step of the race.
