Coach: That is normal, the body adapts, so we need to vary the exercises and add new challenges.
Farhad: Should I add more protein to my diet to support the strength training?
Coach: Yes, but with balanced meals, protein supports recovery, but it is not the only factor.
Farhad: How many repetitions should I do for strength, heavy or moderate weights?
Coach: For strength, use heavier weights with fewer repetitions, and rest well between sets.
Farhad: And how long should I rest between the training days, to avoid injury?
Coach: At least one rest day between strength sessions, and listen to your body, do not push through pain.
Farhad: I will follow this, and how often should we review the plan together?
