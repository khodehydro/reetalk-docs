Coach: That is a difficult period for you, but resting now prevents a longer absence later.
Reza: I understand, but I am worried about losing the progress I have made.
Coach: Do not worry, you can maintain your fitness with exercises the doctor approves.
Reza: The doctor suggested swimming, is that a safe option for my knee?
Coach: Yes, swimming puts less pressure on the knee, and you can swim slowly without strain.
Reza: Then I will go to the pool during these weeks, and keep my routine gentle.
Coach: That is the right approach, and do not return to training before the doctor allows it.
Reza: I will be patient, and I will inform you as soon as I can start training again.
Coach: Good, your health is more important than any training, and we will prepare your return.
Reza: Thank you for your understanding and support during this time.
