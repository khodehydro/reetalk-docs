Parisa: I need to buy some bandages for a small cut on my hand.
Pharmacist: Of course, let me show you the bandages, what size do you need?
Parisa: A small or medium size, the cut is about two centimeters long.
Pharmacist: Medium bandages will suit you, and here are some antiseptic wipes as well.
Parisa: That is helpful, and should I clean the cut before applying the bandage?
Pharmacist: Yes, wash it with clean water, apply the wipe, and then place the bandage.
Parisa: I will follow that, and how often should I change the bandage?
Pharmacist: Change it once a day, or whenever it becomes wet or dirty.
Parisa: If the cut does not heal in a few days, should I see a doctor?
Pharmacist: Yes, if you see redness, swelling, or pain that continues, visit a doctor.
Parisa: I understand, thank you for the advice, I will take these items.
Pharmacist: You are welcome, I hope the cut heals quickly.
