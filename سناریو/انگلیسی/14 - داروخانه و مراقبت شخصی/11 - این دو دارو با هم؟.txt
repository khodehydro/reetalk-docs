Sahar: I take two medicines, and I want to know whether it is safe to take them together.
Pharmacist: Of course, please tell me the names of both medicines, and how you take them.
Sahar: One is for blood pressure, taken in the morning, and the other is for allergies, at night.
Pharmacist: Thank you, let me check, there is a known interaction between these two medicines.
Sahar: Is the interaction serious, and does it affect the effect of either medicine?
Pharmacist: It is a moderate interaction, it can make the blood pressure medicine slightly weaker.
Sahar: What should I do then, should I stop one of the medicines until I ask my doctor?
Pharmacist: Do not stop anything on your own, take them as prescribed, and inform your doctor.
Sahar: Should I take them at different times to reduce the effect of the interaction?
Pharmacist: Separating them by a few hours helps, but the main solution is to consult your doctor.
Sahar: I understand, and is there a way to check the effect on my blood pressure myself?
Pharmacist: Measure your blood pressure regularly at home and note the values, and show them to your doctor.
Sahar: That is practical, and I will book an appointment with my doctor this week.
Pharmacist: That is the right step, and bring both medicine boxes to the appointment.
Sahar: Thank you, I will follow your advice and speak to my doctor soon.
Pharmacist: You are welcome, and your safety is the most important thing here.
