Mona: Every spring, I suffer from sneezing and itchy eyes, and I want some advice.
Pharmacist: Those sound like seasonal allergies, do the symptoms appear every spring?
Mona: Yes, every spring, and the sneezing starts as soon as the flowers begin to bloom.
Pharmacist: That is typical of seasonal allergies, there are medicines that relieve these symptoms.
Mona: What type of medicine do you recommend, and does it need a prescription?
Pharmacist: This antihistamine medicine is available without a prescription and relieves sneezing and itching.
Mona: Is it enough, or should I also take something for the nasal congestion?
Pharmacist: For congestion, a nasal spray works well, it can be used together with the tablets.
Mona: Are there days when the symptoms are worse, and how should I take the medicine then?
Pharmacist: Take the medicine daily during the season, it prevents the symptoms from returning.
Mona: And can I prevent the allergies in the first place, rather than only treating them?
Pharmacist: Keep the windows closed on windy days, and avoid long exposure to the flowers.
Mona: I will follow that, and how long does the season usually last?
Pharmacist: It usually lasts a few weeks in spring, and the medicine will make it much easier.
Mona: Thank you, I feel better knowing there is a clear way to handle this.
Pharmacist: You are welcome, and come back if the symptoms do not improve.
