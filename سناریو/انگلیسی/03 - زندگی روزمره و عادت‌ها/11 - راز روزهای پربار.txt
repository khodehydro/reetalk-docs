Poya: I have noticed that my most productive days are always well planned.
Mina: What is the secret of those days, is it the planning?
Poya: It is, I write my three main tasks in the morning and focus on them.
Mina: What if an unexpected task appears during the day?
Poya: I leave a little time in the afternoon for unexpected things.
Mina: That sounds realistic, most plans fail because they are too strict.
Poya: Exactly, a plan should be a guide, not a prison for you.
Mina: Do you also plan your rest, or do you work until you are tired?
Poya: I plan my rest too, a short walk in the evening clears my mind.
Mina: That is wise, work and rest support each other when they are balanced.
Poya: I have also learned to say no to tasks that do not fit my plan.
Mina: That is a valuable skill, it protects your energy for what matters.
Poya: It does, and my days feel richer and more meaningful now.
Mina: Thank you for sharing, I will try this method next week.
