Farzad: People often argue whether we live to work or work to live.
Leila: It is a deep question, and the answer differs from person to person.
Farzad: For many people, work is only a way to earn money for their life.
Leila: For others, work is where they find meaning and purpose in life.
Farzad: I believe the balance matters more than choosing one side.
Leila: I agree, working hard without time for life is also a kind of loss.
Farzad: And a life without any work can lose its structure and value too.
Leila: True, work gives us routine, skill, and a place in the world.
Farzad: The problem is when work takes over everything else completely.
Leila: Then rest, family, and hobbies disappear, and people feel empty.
Farzad: So we should work with purpose, but never let it consume us.
Leila: A good life is built in the space between work and living.
Farzad: That space is where family, books, and friends find their place.
Leila: Exactly, and protecting that space is a skill everyone must learn.
Farzad: I try to draw a clear line between work time and life time.
Leila: That line may differ for each person, but it must exist.
