Sara: I need to fill in a customs declaration for my package, and I need help with the form.
Clerk: Of course, let me explain the sections, the first part is for the sender and the recipient details.
Sara: I have filled in the sender and recipient, what do I write in the goods description section?
Clerk: Write exactly what the package contains, the quantity, and the value of each item.
Sara: The package contains clothing and a book, and how should I state the value, in which currency?
Clerk: State the value in the local currency, and the amount should be the actual sale value, not an estimate.
Sara: I see, and what about the weight and the dimensions, where should I write those?
Clerk: They are written in the next section, with the exact weight, and the size of the package.
Sara: Is there anything I should not declare, or items that are not allowed to be sent?
Clerk: Items like sharp objects, liquids, and perishable goods cannot be sent, check the list.
Sara: My package has no such items, and once the form is filled, do I attach it to the package?
Clerk: Yes, a copy goes inside the package, and another copy is attached to the outside, clearly visible.
Sara: Thank you for the help, and is there a fee for the customs declaration itself?
Clerk: There is no separate fee for the declaration, the customs charge applies only if the goods are taxed.
Sara: I understand, and I will complete the form carefully, and attach it to the package.
Clerk: You are welcome, and bring the package here, once the declaration is complete, for the final check.
