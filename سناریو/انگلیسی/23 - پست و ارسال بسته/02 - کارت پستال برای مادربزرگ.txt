Sara: I would like to send a postcard to my grandmother, can you help me with that?
Clerk: Of course, do you have the postcard ready, and the address written on it?
Sara: Yes, the postcard is here, and I have written the address on the back.
Clerk: Good, you need a stamp for the postcard, and it goes in the post box, or I can send it for you.
Sara: I will buy a stamp, and can you tell me how much the postage costs for another country?
Clerk: For a postcard to another country, it costs two euros, and the delivery takes about a week.
Sara: Thank you, and should I write anything else on the card, like the date or my message?
Clerk: The message and the date are enough, and make sure the address is clear and complete.
Sara: I have written everything, and where should I post it, or can I hand it to you?
Clerk: You can hand it to me here, and I will post it today, so it reaches your grandmother quickly.
