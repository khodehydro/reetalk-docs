Nima: I would like to send a package to Tehran, and I need to know the process and the cost.
Clerk: Welcome, for an international package, you need to fill in a customs form and weigh the package.
Nima: What should I write on the customs form, and what information is required?
Clerk: You need to describe the contents, state the value, and give the full recipient address.
Nima: The package contains books and some gifts, and how much will the shipping cost?
Clerk: The cost depends on the weight and the destination, and for this size, it is about fifteen euros.
Nima: Is there a weight limit for the package, and what happens if it is over the limit?
Clerk: The limit is twenty kilograms, and over that, the package must be split, or sent as freight.
Nima: And how long will the delivery take, to reach Tehran from here?
Clerk: International delivery takes about two to three weeks, depending on the destination.
Nima: Should I include the invoice or any document inside the package, or is the form enough?
Clerk: Include a copy of the customs form inside, and keep the receipt, as proof of posting.
Nima: I understand, and thank you for the information, I will prepare the package, fill in the form, and track the package once it has been sent.
Clerk: You are welcome, and bring the package here, once it is ready, to send it.
