Behnam: I need to coordinate the shipment of a commercial container, and I would like to discuss the details.

Behnam: The container will be shipped from the port here, to the destination port, and the cargo is machinery parts.
Logistics Officer: Understood, let me check the available routes, the direct route takes about two weeks, the transit is faster.
Behnam: The direct route sounds good, what documents do we need to prepare for the customs clearance?
Logistics Officer: You need the commercial invoice, the packing list, the certificate of origin, and the bill of lading.
Behnam: We have the invoice and the packing list, and how should we handle the customs duties, who pays them?
Logistics Officer: The duties are usually paid by the importer, and we can handle the payment process for you, on request.
Behnam: That would be helpful, and what about the insurance, does the shipment include coverage for the cargo?
Logistics Officer: Basic coverage is included, and for high value cargo, you can take additional insurance, through us.
Behnam: How will the container be tracked, during the voyage, and how will we be informed of its progress?
Logistics Officer: The container has a tracking number, and you can follow its position, at every stage of the voyage.
Behnam: And what happens if there is a delay, due to weather or port congestion, how will we be informed?
Logistics Officer: We will notify you immediately, with the revised schedule, and the new expected time of arrival.
Behnam: Thank you for the information, when should we submit the documents, to book the shipment?
Logistics Officer: Submit the documents at least one week before the departure, so we can complete the booking.
Behnam: I understand, and we will prepare the documents, and contact you to confirm the booking.
Logistics Officer: You are welcome, and we will coordinate every step, to ensure a smooth shipment.
