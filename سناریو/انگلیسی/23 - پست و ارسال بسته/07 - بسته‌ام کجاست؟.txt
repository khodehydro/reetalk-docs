Reza: I have not received my package, and it is late, I would like to trace it.
Clerk: I understand, may I have the tracking number, or the reference of the package?
Reza: The tracking number is nine four two one seven, and the package was sent two weeks ago.
Clerk: Thank you, let me check the system, the package is marked as delivered to your address.
Reza: That cannot be right, I never received it, and I have been home the whole time.
Clerk: There may be a mistake in the delivery record, I will open a trace request for the package.
Reza: How long will the trace take, and how will I be informed of the result?
Clerk: The trace takes about five working days, and we will contact you, by phone or email, with the result.
Reza: And if the package cannot be found, what happens, will I get a refund or a replacement?
Clerk: If the package is declared lost, you will receive a full refund, or a replacement, as you prefer.
Reza: That is fair, and is there anything else I should do, to help with the search?
Clerk: Check with your neighbours, and the building staff, in case it was delivered to them by mistake.
Reza: I will do that, and thank you for opening the trace for my package.
Clerk: You are welcome, and we will keep you informed of every step of the investigation.
