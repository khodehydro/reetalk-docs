Farhad: What information should I write in the claim form, and where do I send it?
Clerk: Write the tracking number, the date of delivery, and describe the damage, then send it to the claims department.

Clerk: The claim must be submitted within seven days, of the delivery, with the evidence attached.
Farhad: And what compensation can I expect, will it be a refund, or a replacement?
Clerk: The compensation depends on the damage, and the value, you can choose a refund, or a replacement.
Farhad: How long does the review take, before I receive the decision on my claim?
Clerk: The review takes about two weeks, and you will receive the decision, by letter or email.
Farhad: I understand, and I will keep the damaged package until the claim is resolved, fill in the form, attach the photographs, and send the claim.
