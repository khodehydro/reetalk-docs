Farhad: I received a yellow notice paper from the post, and I need to collect a package.
Clerk: Welcome, the yellow notice means your package is at the post office, and ready for collection.
Farhad: Where should I go to collect it, and what do I need to bring with me?
Clerk: Go to the collection point, and bring the notice, and your identification, to prove it is yours.
Farhad: I have the notice and my identification, and is there a deadline for collecting the package?
Clerk: Yes, the package is kept for two weeks, and after that, it is returned to the sender.
Farhad: What are the opening hours of the collection point, and is it open on weekends?
Clerk: It is open on weekdays, from eight in the morning, to six in the evening, closed on weekends.
Farhad: And can someone else collect the package for me, if I am unable to come?
Clerk: Yes, but they need your notice, your identification, and a written authorization from you.
Farhad: I see, and if the package is damaged when I receive it, what should I do immediately?
Clerk: Note the damage on the collection receipt, and refuse the package, or report it at once.
Farhad: Thank you for the guidance, I will go to the collection point tomorrow morning.
Clerk: You are welcome, and bring all the documents, so the collection goes smoothly.
