Omid: The post office told me my package was delivered to the neighbour, and I need to collect it.
Clerk: Welcome, the neighbour has the package, and they signed for it on your behalf.
Omid: How can I get the package from the neighbour, and do I need anything with me?
Clerk: You can go to the neighbour, with your identification, and they will hand over the package.
Omid: I see, and if the neighbour is not at home when I go, what should I do?
Clerk: Leave a note with your contact details, and arrange another time that suits both of you.
Omid: Is the neighbour's address the one shown on the delivery notice, and is it nearby?
Clerk: Yes, the address is on the notice, and it is the neighbour next door, a short walk from here.
Omid: What if the package is damaged when the neighbour hands it to me, who should I report it to?
Clerk: Report it to us immediately, with the neighbour as witness, and we will open an investigation.
Omid: And how long do I have to collect the package, before it is returned to the sender?
Clerk: You have two weeks, and if it is not collected, it is returned, and you are notified by letter.
Omid: Thank you for the information, I will go to the neighbour now, with my identification.
Clerk: You are welcome, and if there is any issue, come back to us, and we will help you.
