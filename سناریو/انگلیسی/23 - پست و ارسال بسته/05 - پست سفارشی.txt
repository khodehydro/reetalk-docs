Maryam: I would like to send a registered package, and I need to know the cost and the service.
Clerk: Welcome, for a registered package, the cost depends on the weight and the declared value.
Maryam: How much does a registered package cost, compared to a regular one?
Clerk: A registered package costs more, about ten euros extra, and includes tracking and a signature on delivery.
Maryam: What does the tracking service include, and how do I follow the package?
Clerk: You receive a tracking number, and you can check the package location, at any stage of delivery.
Maryam: Is the signature on delivery required, and what if the recipient is not at home?
Clerk: The signature is required, and if no one is at home, a new delivery is scheduled, or you can arrange a collection point.
Maryam: And does the registered service cover insurance for the contents, if something is lost or damaged?
Clerk: It covers a basic insurance, and for higher value items, you can add extra coverage, at a small cost.
Maryam: How long does the registered delivery take, compared to a regular package?
Clerk: Registered delivery is faster, and takes two to three days domestically, and up to a week abroad.
Maryam: I see, and thank you for the information, I will weigh the package, bring it to send it, and keep the receipt as proof of posting.
Clerk: You are welcome, and make sure the contents are well packed, to avoid any damage.
