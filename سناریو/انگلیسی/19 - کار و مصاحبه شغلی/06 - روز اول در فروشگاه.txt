Omid: This is my first day at the store, and I would like to know my duties.
Trainer: Welcome to the team, let me walk you through the store and explain your duties.
Omid: Thank you, what will I do in the morning, when the store opens?
Trainer: In the morning, you will arrange the shelves, check the products, and prepare the cash register.
Omid: And during the day, how do I assist customers, and where should I be?
Trainer: Stay on the sales floor, assist customers with their needs, and answer their questions.
Omid: What about the stockroom, will I be responsible for the inventory there?
Trainer: Yes, you will help with receiving deliveries and keeping the stockroom organized.
Omid: Is there a break during the shift, and where can I take it?
Trainer: You have a break of half an hour in the middle of the shift, in the staff room.
Omid: If a customer has a complaint, who should I turn to for help?
Trainer: Call the supervisor, she is trained to handle complaints and resolve them.
Omid: I understand, and is there anything else I should remember for the first days?
Trainer: Ask questions whenever you are unsure, that is the best way to learn quickly.
Omid: Thank you, I feel ready, and I am looking forward to working here.
Trainer: You are welcome, we are glad to have you, and we will support you throughout.
