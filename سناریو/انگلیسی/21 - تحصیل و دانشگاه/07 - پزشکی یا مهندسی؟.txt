Reza: I am trying to choose my field of study, and I would like your advice on it.
Counselor: I am happy to help, what are the two fields you are considering?
Reza: I am torn between medicine and engineering, both interest me a great deal.
Counselor: Let us think about your strengths, which field matches your skills and interests better?
Reza: I like both science and design, so it is hard to decide between the two.
Counselor: Consider the nature of the work, medicine is patient care, while engineering is design and building.
Reza: That is the difference, and which field has better prospects in the coming years?
Counselor: Both have good prospects, but engineering is broader, with many different branches.
Reza: What about the difficulty of the studies, and the length of the program?
Counselor: Medicine takes longer, and is demanding, while engineering varies by branch.
Reza: I should think about my personality, not only about the job market, right?
Counselor: Exactly, choose what you will enjoy for years, and what suits your character.
Reza: That is wise, I will reflect on it, and discuss it with my family too.
Counselor: Take your time, and we can meet again, once you have thought it through.
