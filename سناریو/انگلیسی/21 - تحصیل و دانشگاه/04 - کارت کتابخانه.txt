Maryam: I would like to become a member of the university library, what do I need to do?
Librarian: To join, bring your student card, a photo, and fill in this membership form.
Maryam: I have the documents with me, and is there a membership fee for students?
Librarian: No, the library membership is free for all registered students.
Maryam: That is great, how many books can I borrow at a time, and for how long?
Librarian: You can borrow up to five books, for two weeks, and you can renew them once.
Maryam: And if I return the books late, is there a fine, and how much is it?
Librarian: Yes, there is a small fine for late returns, one euro for each day of delay.
Maryam: I understand, and are there study rooms available, where I can work quietly?
Librarian: Yes, we have quiet study rooms on the second floor, which can be reserved in advance.
Maryam: Can I use the library's computers and internet, and is there a limit on printing?
Librarian: Yes, computers and internet are free for members, with a limit of twenty pages of printing per day.
Maryam: That is convenient, and what are the opening hours, I will fill in the form and start using the library right away.
Librarian: The library is open from seven in the morning until ten at night, all week, and I hope it helps you with your studies.
