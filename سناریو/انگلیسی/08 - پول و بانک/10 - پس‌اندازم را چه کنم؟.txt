Niloufar: I have some savings, and I do not know what to do with them.
Advisor: First, think about your goal, are you saving for something specific or for the future?
Niloufar: For the future, I want it to grow safely, I do not want to risk it.
Advisor: Then a savings account or a low-risk fund is the right choice for you.
Niloufar: How much interest can I earn, and is it guaranteed?
Advisor: Savings accounts offer modest but guaranteed returns, higher than a regular account.
Niloufar: Should I put all my savings in one place, or divide them?
Advisor: It is wiser to divide them, keep an emergency fund easily accessible, and invest the rest.
Niloufar: How much should I keep as an emergency fund?
Advisor: Usually three to six months of your expenses, so a sudden need does not trouble you.
Niloufar: And what about the rest, should I invest it for the long term?
Advisor: Yes, the long term allows your savings to grow through compound interest.
Niloufar: That makes sense, I will start with the emergency fund first.
Advisor: A good plan, and review your savings every year to adjust if needed.
Niloufar: Thank you, now I know exactly how to manage my savings.
Advisor: You are welcome, small regular savings build a strong future over time.
