Guest: Could you tell me at what time breakfast is served, please?

Guest: Is breakfast included in the room price, or do I need to pay extra?
Receptionist: Yes, breakfast is included in the rate for this room, there is no extra charge.

Receptionist: There is a buffet with bread, cheese, eggs, fruit, and hot drinks, a wide choice.
Guest: That sounds good, is the restaurant on the same floor as the reception?
Receptionist: No, the restaurant is on the ground floor, just down the stairs from the lobby.
Guest: I understand, and can I have breakfast early, at six thirty, if I have an early departure?
Receptionist: Yes, we can arrange an early breakfast, please tell us the night before.
Guest: Thank you, I will inform you tonight, that is very helpful.
Receptionist: You are welcome, we are happy to accommodate your needs.
