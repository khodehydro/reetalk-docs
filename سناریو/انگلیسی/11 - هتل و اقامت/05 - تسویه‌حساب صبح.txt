Guest: I would like to settle my bill this morning, can I pay at the reception?
Receptionist: Of course, I will prepare your bill, do you want me to check the charges first?
Guest: Yes, please, I want to make sure there are no extra charges on the bill.
Receptionist: Here is the bill, it includes two nights of accommodation and breakfast.
Guest: It looks correct, and there is nothing extra, how would you like me to pay?
Receptionist: You can pay by card or in cash, whichever is more convenient for you.
Guest: I will pay by card, here it is, and here is my room key as well.
Receptionist: Thank you, the payment is complete, I will return your card and give you the receipt.
Guest: Thank you, I have enjoyed my stay, and I may come back again.
Receptionist: We would be happy to welcome you again, and I wish you a good journey.
Guest: Thank you, goodbye, and have a nice day.
