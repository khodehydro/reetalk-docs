Kamran: I have the passport and the form, do I need to bring my flight tickets as well?
Officer: Yes, a confirmed flight reservation is required, along with your travel insurance.
Kamran: I have the insurance, and I have printed the flight reservation, is that enough?
Officer: That is enough, also bring proof of your financial situation, such as bank statements.
Kamran: I will prepare the bank statement today, how long will the interview take?
Officer: The interview is usually short, about fifteen to twenty minutes, be on time.
Kamran: I will arrive half an hour early, and what questions will they ask me?
Officer: They usually ask about the purpose of your trip, your job, and your return plans.
Kamran: I will prepare clear answers, is there anything else I should know?
Officer: Answer honestly and confidently, and keep all your documents organized and complete.
Kamran: Thank you for the guidance, I feel more prepared now.
