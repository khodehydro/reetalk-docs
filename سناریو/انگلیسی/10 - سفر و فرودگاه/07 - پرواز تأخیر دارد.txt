Traveler: My flight has been delayed, can you tell me how long the delay will be?
Agent: I am sorry, the flight is delayed by two hours because of weather conditions.
Traveler: Two hours is a long time, will the departure time change again?
Agent: We will inform you of any change, the new departure time is now eight in the evening.
Traveler: Is there any service for the waiting passengers, like food or refreshments?
Agent: Yes, the airline provides light refreshments and drinks for passengers during the delay.
Traveler: That is good, where can I get them, and will the gate number change?
Agent: The refreshments are served in the waiting area next to gate five, and the gate is still the same.
Traveler: Thank you for the information, I will wait there and follow the announcements.
Agent: You are welcome, we will make an announcement as soon as there is any update.
Traveler: I hope the weather improves quickly, and the flight departs on time.
Agent: We hope so too, and we are sorry again for the inconvenience.
