Farzad: I am applying for a mortgage for my first home, and I want to understand the process.
Advisor: That is a big step, let us go through the main points together.
Farzad: What is the first thing I need to prepare before applying?
Advisor: You need to show stable income, savings for the down payment, and a clean credit record.
Farzad: How much of the house price should I pay as a down payment?
Advisor: Usually ten to twenty percent, the rest is financed by the bank over twenty years.
Farzad: And the monthly payment, does it include insurance and taxes, or are they extra?
Advisor: They are usually extra, so calculate them separately when you budget.
Farzad: What if interest rates change during the loan period?
Advisor: Some loans have fixed rates, others are variable, so read the contract carefully.
Farzad: Is there anything I should avoid during the loan approval period?
Advisor: Avoid new debts or big purchases, they can affect your approval.
Farzad: That is important, I will not change my spending habits until it is approved.
Advisor: That is wise, and pre-approval before house hunting also gives you a clear budget.
Farzad: Thank you, I feel much more confident about this important decision.
Advisor: You are welcome, take your time, and ask more questions before signing anything.
