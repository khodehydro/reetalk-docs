Reza: I am calling about my deposit, the landlord is not returning it.
Advisor: Do you have the contract and the receipt of the deposit payment?
Reza: Yes, I kept everything, the contract says the deposit is returned within one month.
Advisor: The deadline has passed, so you can send him a formal written reminder.
Reza: What should the reminder include, so it is legally effective?
Advisor: Mention the contract date, the deposit amount, and the exact deadline for the return.
Reza: I will send it by registered mail so there is a record of delivery.
Advisor: That is the right step, and give him a few days to respond after it arrives.
Reza: If he still refuses, what can I do next?
Advisor: You can file a complaint with the rental board or take the case to a mediator.
Reza: That sounds serious, but I hope the reminder is enough.
Advisor: Often it is, because landlords know their legal obligations clearly.
Reza: Thank you, I will send the letter today and keep you updated.
Advisor: You are welcome, and keep copies of every document you send.
