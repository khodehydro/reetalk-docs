Sara: Could you tell me how to get to the museum, I am completely lost.
Local: Go straight along this street for two blocks, then turn left at the traffic lights.
Sara: After the turn, how long do I walk before I reach the museum?
Local: Walk for about five minutes, the museum is on your right, in a large building.
Sara: Is there anything distinctive about the building, so I can recognize it easily?
Local: Yes, it is a large red building with a tall clock tower, you cannot miss it.
Sara: That is clear, and should I cross any roads on the way?
Local: Yes, at the traffic lights, be careful, the crossings are marked clearly.
Sara: I understand, and if I get lost again, who can I ask?
Local: You can ask any shopkeeper, they all know the museum well.
Sara: Thank you for the detailed directions, I feel much more confident now.
Local: You are welcome, I am sure you will find it without any problem.
