Traveler: Excuse me, where is the metro station, I am looking for it.
Local: The metro station is just down this street, about two hundred meters ahead.
Traveler: Is it on the left side or the right side of the street?
Local: It is on the right side, you will see the metro sign above the entrance.
Traveler: Thank you, and does this street lead directly to the station?
Local: Yes, just walk straight, you will reach it in about three minutes.
Traveler: That is very helpful, is the ticket office inside the station?
Local: Yes, the ticket office and the machines are both inside, on the left.
Traveler: I understand, thank you for the clear directions.
Local: You are welcome, have a good journey.
