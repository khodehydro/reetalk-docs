Reza: I am worried about where our personal data goes, and I would like to discuss data privacy.
Friend: That is an important topic, what concerns you the most, about how your data is collected?
Reza: Apps and websites ask for many permissions, and I am not sure what they do, with the data.
Friend: The data is mostly used, to show ads, and to personalize services, according to your activity.
Reza: And is my data safe, when I give it to a company, who is responsible, if it is misused?
Friend: Companies are responsible, for protecting the data, and the law requires them, to keep it secure.
Reza: But many breaches happen, how can I know, if a company protects my data, well enough?
Friend: Look for clear privacy policies, and check if they mention encryption, and how they handle breaches.
Reza: Should I avoid giving my data, entirely, or is it enough, to read the terms, before agreeing?
Friend: Reading the key terms, and giving only what is needed, is a practical middle ground, in most cases.
Reza: And what about free services, if the service is free, is my data the product, in that case?
Friend: Often it is, free services rely on data, so think about what you give, for what you get.
Reza: I see, and are there tools, that can help me, protect my data, and limit the collection?
Friend: Yes, privacy settings, tracking blockers, and strong passwords, help a lot, in everyday use.
Reza: That is useful, and I will review my settings, and be more careful, with what I share.
Friend: That is a good step, being informed, and careful, is the best protection, against misuse.
