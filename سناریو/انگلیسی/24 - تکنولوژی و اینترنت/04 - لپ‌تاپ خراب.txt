Omid: My laptop is broken, and I would like to take it to a repair shop, what is the procedure?
Technician: Welcome, first, describe the problem, and when it started, so I can check the device.
Omid: The screen goes black randomly, and it started two days ago, without any damage.
Technician: That may be a problem with the screen, or the connection, let me run a quick diagnostic test.
Omid: Is the repair expensive, and do you need to order parts, which would take time?
Technician: The cost depends on the diagnosis, and if a screen is needed, the part takes about a week to arrive.
Omid: And do you provide a warranty, on the repair, and on the replaced parts?
Technician: Yes, we give a six month warranty, on the repair, and on the new parts we install.
Omid: How long will the repair take, if it needs a new screen, and can I use a loaner laptop meanwhile?
Technician: The repair takes about two days, once the part arrives, and yes, a loaner is available.
Omid: I see, and is it better to repair, or to buy a new laptop, given the age of the device?
Technician: For a newer device, repairing is usually more economical, than replacing it entirely.
Omid: That makes sense, and I will leave the laptop with you, for the repair.
Technician: Very well, I will start the diagnosis, and contact you, with the cost and the time frame.
