Farhad: I have a bad toothache on the left side of my mouth, it is really painful.
Dentist: Thank you for coming, let me examine the tooth, please open your mouth.
Farhad: I have been in pain since last night, and it gets worse when I drink cold things.
Dentist: The tooth has a cavity, the decay has reached the nerve, which causes the pain.
Farhad: That explains the pain, what is the treatment, will it be painful?
Dentist: I will clean the decay and fill the cavity, the injection will numb the area first.
Farhad: I understand, will the procedure take long, and when can I eat afterwards?
Dentist: The filling takes about half an hour, and you should avoid eating for two hours after.
Farhad: All right, and how should I care for the tooth to avoid this again?
Dentist: Brush your teeth twice a day, use fluoride toothpaste, and avoid very sweet foods.
Farhad: I will follow your advice, thank you for the treatment.
Dentist: You are welcome, come back for a checkup in six months to make sure it is fine.
