Reza: I would like some fresh bread for breakfast, what do you have?
Baker: We have oven-fresh bread, still warm, and also baguettes.
Reza: The warm bread smells wonderful, how much is one loaf?
Baker: One loaf is one unit, and the baguette is one unit and a half.
Reza: I will take two loaves of the warm bread, please.
Baker: No problem, I will wrap them in paper so they stay warm.
Reza: Thank you, when do you bake the bread, every morning?
Baker: Yes, we start baking at four in the morning, so the bread is always fresh.
Reza: That is why it tastes so good, I will come early every morning.
Baker: You are welcome, we save fresh bread for our regular customers.
