Reza: Organic vegetables are more expensive, are they really worth the higher price?
Mina: In my opinion, yes, they taste better and have less pesticides.
Reza: But the price difference is large, is it worth it for every purchase?
Mina: Maybe not every time, but for vegetables you eat raw, it matters more.
Reza: That is a good point, I should be more selective about organic purchases.
Mina: Also, local vegetables at the market are often fresh and reasonably priced.
Reza: The market sellers grow their own produce, so the quality is usually good.
Mina: True, and supporting local farmers is a good reason by itself.
Reza: Then a mix of organic and market vegetables is a reasonable choice.
Mina: Exactly, balance is better than choosing only one option always.
Reza: Thank you, I have a clearer idea now about how to shop.
Mina: You are welcome, and the health of the family matters most in the end.
