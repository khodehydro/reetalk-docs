Nima: Excuse me, where can I find the milk in this supermarket?
Employee: Milk is in the cold section, at the far end of the aisle.
Nima: Is there fresh milk as well as packaged milk?
Employee: Yes, fresh milk is on the left shelf, and packaged milk is in the middle.
Nima: Which one do you recommend, I want milk for breakfast.
Employee: The fresh one in the glass bottles is very good for breakfast.
Nima: Thank you, and how much is one bottle?
Employee: One bottle is two units, and they are in the left corner of the shelf.
Nima: Perfect, I will go and get one right now.
Employee: You are welcome, ask me if you need help finding anything else.
