Omid: I would like to debate, where the limits, of free speech, should lie, in a free society.
Guest: Thank you, my position, is that free speech, should be broadly protected, and not silenced, by the state.
Omid: But you agree, that some limits, are necessary, to protect, people, and the public, from harm?
Guest: Yes, limits, such as incitement to violence, and defamation, are justified, and do not weaken, free speech.
Omid: And what about, hate speech, that targets, groups of people, where should the line, be drawn, there?
Guest: Speech that incites, hatred, and violence, against groups, should be limited, to protect, their dignity, and safety.
Omid: Critics say, that such limits, can be used, by those in power, to silence, their opponents, and critics.
Guest: That is a real risk, which is why, limits must be, clear, narrow, and applied, by an independent judiciary.
Omid: Does the media, and social platforms, have a role, in setting, these limits, or is it, only the law?
Guest: Platforms set their own rules, but the law, must set, the boundaries, and protect, free expression, overall.
Omid: Some argue, that free speech, can itself, cause harm, to individuals, and to groups, is that a concern?
Guest: It can, but the answer, is more speech, and counter argument, not censorship, that is the democratic way.
Omid: And in practice, how do we distinguish, between speech, that should be protected, and speech, that should be limited?
Guest: By clear, legal tests, focused, on concrete harm, not on vague, or offensive, ideas, and opinions.
Omid: Thank you, for this exchange, and I think, the discussion, shows, both the value, and the difficulty, of the issue.
Guest: Thank you, and free speech, is precious, but it must be balanced, with responsibility, and respect, for others.
