Maryam: Hello, I am doing a street interview, for the radio, may I ask you a few questions?
Citizen: Of course, I am happy to help, what would you like to ask, about?
Maryam: First, how do you usually get your news, from the television, or from online sources?
Citizen: Mostly from my phone, online, I read the news, and watch short videos, in the morning.
Maryam: And do you trust the news, you find online, or do you have doubts, about what you read?
Citizen: I have some doubts, I try to check, the well known sources, but it takes time, to be sure.
Maryam: What kind of news, matters most, to you, in your daily life, and why is it important?
Citizen: Local news matters most, it affects my neighbourhood, my street, and the people around me.
Maryam: Have you ever shared a story, that later turned out to be false, and how did you feel, about it?
Citizen: Yes, once, and I felt sorry, and I learned, to verify, before sharing, anything.
Maryam: And do you think, people are more informed, now, with all the online sources, or more confused?
Citizen: More informed, but also more confused, there is so much information, and not all of it is true.
Maryam: That is an interesting view, and one last question, what advice, would you give, to a young person?
Citizen: Be patient, and check, before you share, and do not believe, everything, you read, immediately.
Maryam: Thank you very much, for your time, and your answers, they will be part of our report.
Citizen: You are welcome, and I am glad, to take part, in your programme.
