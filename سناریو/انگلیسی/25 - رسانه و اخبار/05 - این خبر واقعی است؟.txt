Reza: I saw a news story online, and I am not sure, whether it is true, or not, how can we tell?
Friend: That is a real problem, fake news spreads fast, and many people share it, without checking.
Reza: What are the signs, that a story is not reliable, and where should we look, to verify it?
Friend: Check the source, is it a known, trustworthy outlet, and see if other reliable sources, report the same.
Reza: And what about the headlines, that are shocking, and make you want to click, immediately?
Friend: Sensational headlines, are a warning sign, they often exaggerate, to attract attention, and clicks.
Reza: Some stories include no date, or no author, is that also a sign, that we should be careful?
Friend: Yes, a credible story, has a clear source, a date, and an author, and you can trace the origin.
Reza: What about images, and videos, can they be fake, and how can we check, if they are real?
Friend: Images can be taken out of context, or manipulated, look for inconsistencies, and search for the image online.
Reza: And if a story is emotional, and makes me react strongly, should I be more cautious, before sharing?
Friend: Exactly, emotional reactions, are exactly, what fake news exploits, so pause, and verify, before sharing.
Reza: Is there a quick check, we can do, before sharing, a story, with friends and family?
Friend: Ask, who benefits from this story, and whether you would trust it, if it came from a known source.
Reza: That is practical, and I will slow down, and check the source, before I share, any news.
Friend: That habit protects you, and your contacts, from spreading, what may turn out to be false.
