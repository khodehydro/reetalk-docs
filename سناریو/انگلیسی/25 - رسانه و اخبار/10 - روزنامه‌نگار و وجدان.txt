Reza: I am interviewing a journalist, about ethics in media, and I would like to start, with your view.
Journalist: Thank you, ethics in media, is about accuracy, fairness, and responsibility, to the audience.
Reza: In your experience, what are the hardest ethical decisions, a journalist has to make, in practice?
Journalist: The hardest ones, involve balancing, the public's right to know, with the harm, that publishing can cause.
Reza: And how do you decide, when reporting a sensitive story, what details, to include, and what to leave out?
Journalist: We ask, what the public needs, to know, to understand the story, and avoid details, that only add harm.
Reza: Do you think, the pressure, to be fast, and to attract attention, affects, the quality, of journalism?
Journalist: It does, speed, and sensationalism, can lead to errors, and that is why accuracy, must come first.
Reza: What role, should a journalist's own opinions, play, in their reporting, and where is the line?
Journalist: In news reporting, opinions must be separated, from facts, and analysis, should be clearly labelled, as such.
Reza: And when a source, asks to be anonymous, how do you weigh, that against, the public's right to know?
Journalist: Anonymity is granted, when the source would face, real harm, otherwise, and it must be explained, to readers.
Reza: Have you ever had to correct, a mistake, publicly, and how should media, handle errors, responsibly?
Journalist: Yes, and correcting openly, and quickly, is not a weakness, it is part of, being accountable.
Reza: Do you see, any new challenges, from the digital age, that make ethical journalism, harder, today?
Journalist: The speed, of online news, and the flood, of false information, make verification, harder, and more important.
Reza: What would you say, to someone, who wants to be a journalist, in this environment, today?
Journalist: Be curious, be patient, and hold on, to the standards, of accuracy, and fairness, above all.
Reza: Thank you, for your thoughtful answers, and for sharing, your experience, with our audience.
Journalist: Thank you, and I hope, the discussion, helps people, think about media, more critically.
