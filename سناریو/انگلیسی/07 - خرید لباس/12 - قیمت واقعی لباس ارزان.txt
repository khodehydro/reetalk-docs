Farzad: This cheap shirt looks good at first, but what is the real cost of such cheap clothes?
Leila: There is often a hidden cost, in quality, in the workers who make them, and in the environment.
Farzad: The fabric is thin and the stitching is weak, it will not last more than a few months.
Leila: Exactly, cheap clothes are often used few times and then thrown away.
Farzad: And the people who make them may not be paid fairly for their work.
Leila: That is right, fast fashion relies on very low costs somewhere in the production chain.
Farzad: I never thought about it that way, the low price is always paid by someone.
Leila: Not always, but a very low price is a signal to ask questions.
Farzad: So should we always buy expensive clothes, is that the solution?
Leila: Not necessarily, we should buy fewer, better, and longer-lasting clothes.
Farzad: That makes sense, quality matters more than the number of items we own.
Leila: Yes, a good garment that lasts years is better than ten cheap ones.
Farzad: I will be more careful with my next purchase, and check the material.
Leila: That is a good start, and it is a choice that matters for everyone.
Farzad: I will also share this way of thinking with my friends and family.
Leila: The more people think this way, the better the effect will be.
