Nima: I need to get an identification card, and I would like to know what documents I need.
Officer: To apply for the card, you need to bring your birth certificate, a photo, and proof of address.
Nima: I have the birth certificate and the photo, what counts as proof of address?
Officer: A recent utility bill, or a rental contract, with your name and the address on it.
Nima: I have a rental contract, and how long does it take to get the card after applying?
Officer: The card is ready within two weeks, and we will send it to you by mail.
Nima: Is there a fee for the card, and how do I pay it?
Officer: The card costs ten euros, and you can pay at the cashier here, or online.
Nima: That is clear, and if I lose the card later, what should I do?
Officer: Report it immediately, and apply for a replacement, which takes a shorter time.
Nima: And is the card valid for a long time, or does it need to be renewed?
Officer: The card is valid for ten years, and it must be renewed when the period ends.
Nima: I understand, and thank you for the information, I will prepare the documents and come back to apply, using the card as proof of identity for other services.
