Sara: I am attending the neighbourhood council meeting, and I would like to raise an issue about the area.
Chair: Welcome, you may speak when the agenda reaches the public comment section, what is your concern?
Sara: The street lights on our street have been broken for weeks, and it is unsafe at night.
Chair: Thank you for raising this, and have you reported it to the local maintenance department?
Sara: I reported it once, but nothing has been done, and the situation has not improved.
Chair: I will add this to the priority list, and we will contact the department this week about the repairs.
Sara: That would be great, and is there a time frame, when the repairs might begin?
Chair: We expect a response within a week, and if the work is not scheduled, we will escalate the matter.
Sara: Also, the park near the school is in poor condition, could that be addressed as well?
Chair: Certainly, the park will be included in the same request, as part of the area improvements.
Sara: And will the meeting outcomes, and our requests, be shared with the residents?
Chair: Yes, a summary of the meeting will be posted, and residents will be informed of the progress.
Sara: Thank you, and how can other residents contribute to these improvement efforts?
Chair: Residents can join the volunteer group, and report issues, to help maintain the area.
Sara: I will share this with my neighbours, and encourage them to take part.
Chair: That is welcome, and your participation strengthens the council's work for the neighbourhood.
