Maryam: I have a driving license from my country, and I would like to exchange it for a local one.
Officer: Welcome, to exchange your foreign license, you need to submit some documents and pass a test.
Maryam: Which documents do I need, and is there a fee for the exchange?
Officer: You need the license itself, a translated version, a photograph, and your residence permit, and the fee is twenty euros.
Maryam: I have all the documents, and what test do I need to pass, a written test or a practical one?
Officer: You need to pass a practical test, which is scheduled through our office, after the documents are accepted.
Maryam: How long is the test, and when can I book it, after submitting the documents?
Officer: The test takes about an hour, and you can book it within a week of your application.
Maryam: Is the exchange completed immediately after the test, or does it take more time?
Officer: After passing the test, the license is processed within ten days, and then collected here.
Maryam: And if I fail the test, can I take it again, and is there an extra fee?
Officer: Yes, you can retake it, and there is a small fee for each additional attempt.
Maryam: I understand, and I will prepare the documents and come back to start the process.
Officer: That is good, and we will guide you through every step, once you return.
