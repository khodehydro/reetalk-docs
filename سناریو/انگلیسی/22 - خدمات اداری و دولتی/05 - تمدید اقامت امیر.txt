Farhad: I need to renew my residence permit, and I would like to know the procedure.
Officer: Of course, to renew the permit, you need to fill in this application form and submit it.
Farhad: What documents are required, and is there a fee for the renewal?
Officer: You need your current permit, a photograph, proof of income, and the fee is twenty euros.
Farhad: I have all the documents, and how long does the renewal take, from submission to approval?
Officer: The renewal takes about one month, and you will be notified when the new permit is ready.
Farhad: Is there anything I should do during this period, while the renewal is being processed?
Officer: Keep your old permit with you, as proof, until the new one arrives.
Farhad: And if there is a problem with my application, how will I be informed?
Officer: We will contact you by phone or email, if any additional document is needed.
Farhad: I understand, and can I travel abroad while the renewal is still in process?
Officer: It is not recommended, wait until the new permit is issued, to avoid any issue at the border.
Farhad: That is important to know, and where can I check the status of my application?
Officer: You can check the status online, using the reference number we give you at submission.
Farhad: Thank you for the clear explanation, I will submit the application as soon as possible.
Officer: You are welcome, and I hope the renewal process goes smoothly for you.
