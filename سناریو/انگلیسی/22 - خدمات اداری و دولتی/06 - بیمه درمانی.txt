Omid: I would like to register for health insurance, and I would like to know how to do it.
Officer: Welcome, to register, you need to fill in this form and provide some personal documents.
Omid: What documents are needed, and is there a monthly fee for the insurance?
Officer: You need your identification, a photograph, and proof of income, and the fee depends on your income level.
Omid: I have the documents, and does the insurance cover the whole family, or only the main applicant?
Officer: You can include your family in the policy, and the fee increases according to the number of members.
Omid: And what medical services does the insurance cover, are all treatments included?
Officer: It covers doctor visits, hospital treatment, medicines, and emergency care, with some conditions.
Omid: Is there a waiting period, before the insurance becomes effective, after registration?
Officer: The insurance becomes effective immediately after registration, with no waiting period.
Omid: That is good, and how do I use the insurance, when I visit a doctor or a hospital?
Officer: You present your insurance card, and the provider handles the coverage directly.
Omid: And if I need treatment abroad, is the insurance valid in other countries?
Officer: It is valid in other countries, within the European region, with prior authorization for planned treatment.
Omid: I understand, and thank you for the information, I will fill in the form, complete the registration, and wait for the insurance card at my address.
Officer: You are welcome, and if you have any question about the coverage, we are here to help.
