Sara: I have moved to a new apartment, and I need to register my new address at the municipality.
Clerk: Welcome, to register your address, you need to fill in this form and provide some documents.
Sara: What documents do I need, and is there a fee for the registration?
Clerk: You need your lease contract, your identification, and a recent photograph, and the fee is five euros.
Sara: I have all of them, and how long does the registration take, can I get a receipt today?
Clerk: The registration takes about fifteen minutes, and you will receive a receipt right after it is done.
Sara: That is quick, and when will the new address be updated in the system?
Clerk: The address is updated immediately, and it will be reflected in all the official records.
Sara: Good, and should I inform other offices about the address change as well?
Clerk: Yes, you should update your address with the bank, and any other institutions you deal with.
Sara: I will do that, and is there anything else I need to do to complete the registration?
Clerk: No, once the form is submitted and the fee is paid, the registration is complete.
Sara: Thank you for your help, and I will fill in the form now.
Clerk: You are welcome, and once you are done, bring the form back to me for the final check.
