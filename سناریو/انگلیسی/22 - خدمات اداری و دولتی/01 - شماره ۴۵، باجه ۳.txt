Reza: Good morning, I would like to get a queue number, where do I take it?
Clerk: The queue machine is just beside the entrance, take a number and wait for it to be called.
Reza: Thank you, and how do I know which counter to go to when my number is called?
Clerk: Your number will appear on the screen, along with the counter number to go to.
Reza: I see, and how long is the usual wait, is it long on busy days?
Clerk: It varies, on busy days it can take up to half an hour, but it moves quickly.
Reza: That is fine, and do I need any documents with me for the service today?
Clerk: It depends on the service, but bring your identification, to be safe.
Reza: I have it with me, and if my number is called while I am away, will I lose my place?
Clerk: Yes, if your number is called twice without a response, you lose your place.
Reza: I understand, I will stay near the screen and listen for my number.
Clerk: Good, and when you reach the counter, the officer will guide you through the process.
